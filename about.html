WHO WE ARE

One local point of contact for travel and support.

Amigo Travel Logistics & Tourism supports individuals, families and organizations with travel planning and practical coordination. From our base at Rond-point Express in Yaoundé, we help connect enquiries with the right travel, transport, cargo, production or event support.

Every request is different. We start by listening to your destination, timing and priorities, then discuss the services and arrangements that may fit your plans.
